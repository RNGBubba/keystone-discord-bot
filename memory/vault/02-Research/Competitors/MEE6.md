# MEE6

**Position:** the default bot. Best onboarding, most aggressive paywall.

## Pricing (2026)

$11.99/mo, $49.99/yr, $89.99 lifetime — **per server**, including lifetime. AI is
a separate subscription. No refunds per the current review coverage; the "30% off"
banner has reportedly run for years.

## What is free vs paid

| Free | Paid |
| --- | --- |
| Basic welcome messages | Moderation commands, automod ("Automations") |
| 3 role rewards | Leveling configuration beyond the basics, custom level-up messages, XP multipliers |
| Starboard | Custom commands (up to 500), reaction roles (40), temp voice (100 channels) |
| One dashboard, familiar UX | Embeds (500), social alerts, stat channels, scheduled messages |

MEE6's own comparison table marks moderation, custom commands, economy, giveaways,
levels, welcome and automations as premium. The free tier is a demo.

Music was removed in 2023 after the YouTube crackdown.

## What they do well

- **Onboarding.** The dashboard is the most beginner-friendly in the category.
- **Level cards.** The XP card is the best-looking artefact in the space.
- **Distribution.** Being the default is a moat no feature closes.

## Where it is weak

- **Retroactive re-gating** is the single most-cited complaint: settings admins
  already configured become read-only unless they pay.
- Per-server pricing, including lifetime, makes multi-server communities pay twice.
- No reaction roles or moderation on free, so it cannot be the only bot.
- Separate AI subscription for a feature competitors bundle.

## What we take

- The onboarding clarity: our `/setup` must be as obvious.
- The level card as a design bar; our planned card is a generated image plus a V2 panel, both inside the 40-slot budget once `packages/ui` exists.

## What we do differently

- Free core, permanently, with re-gating banned by [[Non-Negotiables]].
- One flat hosting price, unlimited servers.
- Leveling and moderation in the same free product.

## Caveat

Pricing figures are from a 2026 third-party teardown and MEE6's help centre; the
official pricing page is JS-rendered and could not be read directly. Re-verify
before quoting in marketing.
