# Code of conduct

## Short version

Be decent. Critique code, not people. Assume the other person is trying.

## Expected

- Give feedback on the work: "this guard runs after the database call" beats
  "this is wrong".
- Explain your reasoning, and say when you are unsure.
- Accept "no, because…" as a complete answer. Scope control is maintenance.
- Credit the people whose ideas you used.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, slurs, sexual attention or sustained disruption.
- Personal attacks on skill level. This is a teaching project; beginners are the
  audience, not a problem.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Bad-faith participation: sea-lioning, relitigating settled ADRs without new
  evidence, or demanding unpaid work.

## Scope

This applies in the repository, issues, pull requests, the support server and
any space where you represent the project.

## Enforcement

Report a concern privately to the maintainers through GitHub (the repository
owner is `@RNGBubba`). Do not open a public issue about a person.

Consequences range from a warning, to a temporary ban from the repository, to a
permanent ban, depending on severity and pattern. Maintainers recuse themselves
from decisions about reports involving them. We will not publish a detailed
enforcement log, but we will tell the reporter what happened.
